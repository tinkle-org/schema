# Payments and premium

Premium belongs to the signed-in Tinkle account. iOS sells it through Apple; Android sells it through Google Play. Web can display existing coverage and the originating stores, but has no premium purchase path at launch. No card form, external payment link, `SubmitPayment`, or fallback on verification failure may sell digital premium. The store shows its own price and terms and alone initiates the charge. Verification, restore and receipt reads never charge.

## Start one checkout

1. Read `GetMyEntitlements` on the account's trusted environment. Show **all** verified billing sources, their originating store, coverage and renewal setting. Active Apple coverage on Android means “manage in Apple,” not “buy on Play.” Covered or unresolved accounts get no `checkout_fence`. Unknown eligibility, an offline cache, or an unsupported platform cannot authorize a purchase.
2. On an explicit buy action, save a fresh request UUID and call `CreateInvoice` with the fence and exactly one native product. The server resolves the approved app and product/plan, authenticates the fence, and verifies its account, environment, revision and expiry against current state. Serialize this check, exact-request deduplication, the cross-store coverage/unresolved check, reservation, durable owner binding and revision advance as **one decision per account, environment and premium benefit**. A per-device, per-SKU or per-store lock is insufficient. Verification, trusted notifications and reservation resolution participate in the same serialization. Two eligible reads cannot produce two winning intents.
3. Only the first accepting reply contains `store_intent`. Match invoice origin, SKU, binding case and both deadlines. For Apple, pass the server-issued UUID as StoreKit's [`appAccountToken`](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreserverapi/appaccounttoken). For Play, pass the server-issued opaque account pseudonym via `setObfuscatedAccountId` when launching the billing flow for the configured product and plan. Never derive these values from a user ID, email, or an identifier proposed by the client. The server retains the mapping independently of the client and of the intent's launch lifetime.
4. Consume that reply once for the live buy action: durably mark the intent used **before** invoking the native UI. Ignore duplicate deliveries, replies after sign-out, superseded screens, expiry or uncertain completion. Never relaunch from a saved response. A replay of the original request can recover the invoice but not launch material; changing arguments under the UUID is an error. An unaccepted delayed request with an expired fence or obsolete revision fails even if eligibility has since returned. Neither a new UUID with an old fence nor an old UUID with a new fence may reacquire a slot.
5. Persist a verification request UUID and send it with native evidence to `VerifyStorePurchase`. Include `intent_id` when available. A supplied wrong intent fails; never downgrade a mismatch to an unbound claim. An expired intent prevents **launch**, not verification of a legitimate delayed settlement. A pending/deferred purchase is not a receipt or access. For Ask to Buy with no signed transaction yet, wait for evidence before calling Verify. Only finish a StoreKit transaction or acknowledge a purchased Play subscription after durable verified processing; retry acknowledgement separately, not checkout.

Expiry, a dropped response, an empty device purchase query and dismissing a sheet are not proof that a store cannot settle. Once a launch permit has escaped, keep the slot unresolved across its deadline until matched settlement or authoritative final nonpurchase/cancellation resolves it. If the store cannot establish finality, keep checkout blocked and route to support/reconciliation rather than timing out into a new charge. Never clear a different or newer reservation when late evidence arrives. Releasing a slot advances the revision; deadlines and old fences never reset. A new deliberate checkout requires a fresh eligibility read and new UUID. The contract coordinates Tinkle-initiated checkout; it cannot serialize purchases initiated independently at an external store.

## Restore without an intent, request key or original device

On any device, first read `GetMyEntitlements` and page `ListPayments`. Existing grants do not require proving the purchase again. To import missing facts, restore with StoreKit or query owned Play purchases on the corresponding platform; submit the signed transaction or purchase token with a new verification UUID and `intent_id` **absent**. The server independently verifies store evidence, catalog product, bundle/package, store environment, current lifecycle and the returned platform account binding. Resolve to the durable server-issued binding or an already verified, uniquely owned lineage. Require all available signals to agree. Possessing a token, order number, invoice or logged-in session is not proof of ownership. Never assign an unbound transaction to the first caller.

No client need be running: verify App Store Server Notifications and Play RTDN, fetch authoritative signed transaction/history or Developer API state, and feed the **same** ownership, deduplication and projection rules used by restore. Notifications are wakeups, not credits. Recover missed notifications by server reconciliation with the origin. For a Play out-of-app resubscription, verify and preserve the returned `outOfAppPurchaseContext` (expired token/account identifiers) against the prior durable binding **before** acknowledgement can remove it. For Apple use the verified account token or already-owned original transaction. If no unique trusted mapping exists, quarantine for privileged reconciliation, with no owner-visible grant or record until resolved. Return the same uninformative failure for foreign, unbound and unknown evidence/IDs; never name an owner or reveal that a purchase exists. A mismatched requester cannot change the rightful owner's state.

| Origin | One transaction in the ledger | One subscription lineage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple | Verified `transactionId`; restore, copies of a JWS and repeated notifications do not mint another row. A renewal has a new transaction ID. | `originalTransactionId`, including successive renewals. The app account token binds an owner; it is not a transaction or subscription ID. |
| Play | Verified successful order/line item, e.g. `lineItems[].latestSuccessfulOrderId` for the launch subscription. The same purchase token can have many renewal orders. No verified order means do not invent a settled payment. Backfill missing orders from authoritative history, not by incrementing a suffix. | Map token to an internal lineage. Resolve verified `linkedPurchaseToken` on replacement and verified previous-purchase context on resubscription; reconcile predecessor/successor so overlapping periods do not stack. Do not infer a link from a shared SKU or email. |

Scope origin identities by store, app and verified environment, uniquely across accounts (not per claimant). Keep a durable transaction-to-payment mapping distinct from lineage-to-owner and lineage-to-source mappings. Verify the current origin state before applying a delayed or replayed event: an old signed purchase must not undo a revocation or replace newer renewal evidence. If ownership or chronology conflicts, reconcile before changing coverage. See Google's [subscription purchase fields](https://developers.google.com/android-publisher/api-ref/rest/v3/purchases.subscriptionsv2).

## Recover a command after a lost reply

| Original command | Read as the same actor in the same partition | Successful read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SubmitPayment` | `GetPayment.submit_payment_request_id` | `payment`: the same ledger row, including while settlement is pending. |
| `VerifyStorePurchase` | `GetPayment.verify_store_purchase_request_id` | `verification`: the same typed result as the command's `result`, plus **current** `entitlements`. `pending` needs no payment ID. |
| `CreateRefund` | `GetRefund.create_refund_request_id` | `refund`: the originally accepted refund, including while pending. |

Use exactly one selector. With a known `payment_id` or `refund_id`, read that object directly. Do not search other methods or accounts for a UUID, treat an unknown key as pending, or place another charge after a lookup miss. If delivery is uncertain, retry the original command with unchanged arguments and UUID.

On verification acceptance, atomically bind `(actor, partition, VerifyStorePurchase, request_id)` to the **exact input** (evidence case and bytes, intent presence and value) and the matched purchase occurrence. Retain a protected comparison digest and durable outcome binding; don't log or return the evidence. Concurrent exact retries share one operation. Changed input under the same key fails without effects. Consult this binding **before selecting a transaction** on retry. Independently deduplicate the authoritative transaction: two UUIDs accepted for the same store transaction point to the same payment and receipt, including after expiry, refund or revocation. Bindings never expire into reusable UUIDs. A notification needs no client UUID.

For example, Play token T settles order A under key K. When T later renews as B, a retry or lookup of K still returns A, even if the latest Play query reports B. A new verification or notification can record B as a distinct payment. If K was accepted pending before an order ID existed, freeze the verified attempt/period context on acceptance; resolve it only with evidence linking settlement to **that** occurrence (for example, the initial order in verified history for a pending signup). If history is ambiguous, leave it pending for reconciliation rather than substituting the latest successful order. A definitive nonpurchase yields `not_completed`, without a ledger receipt. A settled outcome never moves back to pending or nonpurchase.

Every successful verification reply and verify-UUID recovery returns the account's current `MyEntitlements`, including for pending or revoked purchases. Use the typed result to settle the operation and the snapshot for current access. Do not fulfill from `verified_payment`, from the existence of a receipt, or from a historical snapshot replayed under the same key.

## Compute and recover current state

For each verified lineage, keep its origin, product, coverage deadline and separate renewal setting. `premium_until` is the latest **currently valid** covered deadline in this account and partition, never the sum. A verified store grace period counts only through its verified deadline. Pending, expired, paused/on-hold and revoked periods do not contribute. Turning renewal off preserves paid-through access; a refund changes the payment ledger, while verified revocation ends that source's coverage at the origin's effective time. Recompute the aggregate: an independently covered source still grants premium. Show both bills if both exist; do not silently cancel, auto-refund or transfer either. Clients expire cached coverage at its deadline and refetch; servers authorize features using current state, not client clocks or snapshots.

Production rejects sandbox, StoreKit local tests, Play test purchases and unknown environments. Lab tests use isolated app configuration, verification credentials, bindings, ledgers, reservations, revisions and entitlement caches. No client field can select production, and a lab grant never unlocks production. Reauthorize before returning even a duplicate outcome. Keep JWS, purchase tokens, native bindings, checkout fences, provider tokens and form handles out of ledger rows, receipts, updates, URLs, logs and analytics; restrict and protect verifier-side evidence. Clear private client state on account or partition change.

`PaymentChanged(payment_id, revision)` and `RefundChanged(refund_id, revision)` ask the owner to refetch `GetPayment` or `GetRefund`. `MyEntitlementsChanged(revision)` asks the receiving account to refetch `GetMyEntitlements` in its current partition. None is a snapshot, receipt, grant, revocation or checkout permit. Coalesce direct and live hints by the highest revision within that scope. Skip a read only if an authorized snapshot is already at least that fresh; a hint alone never advances the applied revision. If a newer hint arrives during a read, refresh again unless the returned snapshot covers it.

On reconnect read the current entitlement snapshot, scan `ListPayments` in bounded pages (a cursor fixes the insertion horizon and is bound to the principal/partition), and follow `refund_id` to `GetRefund`. Restart from the first page after cursor expiry and refetch known IDs with `GetPayment`/`GetPaymentReceipt`; front-page refresh discovers newer insertions. Use the keyed reads above for outstanding commands: a scan cannot recover a pending verification with no payment row. Neither a revision nor an operation ID is a replay cursor.

## Other invoices and refunds

`CreateInvoice.other_order_id` references a server-priced, separately authorized non-store order, not a client product label or an amount. Require the same non-premium policy on `GetPaymentForm`, `ValidatePaymentDetails` and `SubmitPayment`, including retries. Bind a form and its validated handle to actor, order, immutable invoice terms, provider and expiry. Validation only exchanges a token for a handle; it does not charge. Atomically claim the order once across invoices, handles and request IDs. Bind the submit UUID to its exact input before contacting the provider and use the same provider idempotency key after uncertainty. Recover pending settlement, never charge again because of a timeout. Receipts require confirmed original settlement. Only `CreateRefund` for an authorized other invoice may request a full refund; bind its UUID and serialize by payment so the same funds are not returned twice. `GetRefund` reports the outcome. For a store purchase direct the user to the origin; import a verified store reversal, never invoke a generic refund, revoke on request, or promise store approval.

A refund never rewrites original settlement facts: `Payment.state` stays `SUCCEEDED`, and the original payment ID, settled amount/currency and receipt's `settled_at` stay unchanged. `Payment.refund_id` links to the separate `Refund.state`; only the origin confirms a successful refund. Read `MyEntitlements` for access after revocation.

## Cross-store release tests

These are gates for the implementing service and native clients, **not** results of the descriptor tests. Exercise both directions where two platforms appear. Use isolated store test environments and controlled production-verifier rejection fixtures.

| Exercise | Required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Apple purchase → Android/web login; Play purchase → iOS/web login | Same account gets one premium, same deadline and originating store; no second checkout, card path or web checkout. Wrong account learns nothing. |
| Two devices, opposite stores and different UUIDs submit against one eligible revision; repeat with same-store SKUs | Exactly one launch response, one reservation. Duplicate winning UUID cannot launch again. Reusing it with changed input fails. |
| Drop reserve reply, duplicate delayed reply, expire permit/fence, replay after release and after another reservation | No retry extends or issues a permit. Pending settlement still blocks both stores. Delayed first arrival with old fence fails; late resolution does not clear newer work. |
| Ask to Buy / Play pending, delayed approval, rejection or provider outage | No pending grant, receipt, finish/ack or second checkout. Verified success resolves once; unresolved outage stays blocked. |
| Reinstall or lose the entire client request and intent; notification before client, client before notification, notification only | Same verified row and lineage; restore without intent and startup reads converge. Unbound/conflicting evidence is quarantined; supplied wrong intent is rejected. |
| Apple renewal and replay; Play renewal on same token, replacement on a linked token, out-of-app resubscription | Distinct verified orders/transactions become distinct rows; stable verified lineage, one grant, no dropped renewal or double fulfillment. |
| Verify order A with two keys, lose both replies, refund/revoke A, then renew the same Play token as B | Both UUID lookups and exact retries resolve A's original settlement and receipt, not B. Each returns current coverage. Changed evidence or intent under either key fails. |
| Lose a pending verify reply before an order exists; then receive ambiguous history, matched settlement, or final cancellation | UUID lookup returns `pending` without a payment. Only a matched settlement resolves to its payment; definitive nonpurchase resolves to `not_completed`. No terminal result rebinds to a later order. |
| Use the same UUID on Submit and Verify, lose a refund reply, and miss all live hints | Method-specific lookups recover their own outcomes. Refund UUID resolves the original refund. Repeated or late hints prompt scoped reads; none changes settlement or grants access. |
| Independent paid Apple and Play sources; turn off renewal, refund/revoke either, deliver old success last, then expire survivor | Display both, keep paid-through on cancellation, only survivor contributes after revocation, no stale regrant. No premium after final expiry. |
| Foreign proof/IDs and lab evidence against production, including colliding transaction IDs | No disclosure, rebinding, acknowledgment, production ledger mutation or production coverage. No evidence in receipts/updates/telemetry. |
| Disguised premium order, swapped handles, duplicate non-store submit/refund and store `CreateRefund` | Policy checked on every route, one authorized charge/refund at most. Store refund attempt has no effect; verified origin reversal is discoverable without the update. |
