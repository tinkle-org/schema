# Group calls

`tinkle.v1.CallsService` is the service for both direct and group calls. In a group call, the roster tells you who is participating. It does not give a device the connection details it needs to send or receive media.

## From invitation to participation

- **Schedule or start.** Each call belongs to a group chat or channel. A scheduled call can be started or cancelled; reaching its scheduled time does not start it. A reminder is a personal preference for that call; enabling it does not join the call or guarantee a notification. Even the person who starts a call must join separately.
- **Invite and join.** An invitation identifies a call the recipient can ask to join. It does not add the recipient to the roster, authorize media, or turn on a device. Joining establishes membership; negotiating the media connection is a separate step.
- **Recover after disconnect.** If the join reply is lost, use `GetGroupCallConnection` with the call ID on the same device. It returns the current session if the device is still joined; it cannot join on the device's behalf or take over another device's membership. Refresh the call and roster separately for shared state.
- **Leave or end.** Leaving relinquishes that membership; ending a live call closes it for everyone. A rejoin gets a new `participant_id` even for the same account. Never retry a command aimed at the old participation against the new one.

## Negotiate with the SFU

Each participating device negotiates with a selective forwarding unit (SFU), not with every other participant. Set the relay-only policy before gathering ICE candidates. After gathering completes, send the device's **full SDP offer** and track mappings to `NegotiateGroupCallConnection`. Apply the SFU's **full SDP answer** only to the matching session and offer sequence. Both descriptions include the candidates and end-of-candidates markers; there is no group trickle inbox.

Keep one exchange outstanding at a time. If the reply is lost, retry the latest offer with the same sequence, SDP bytes, and mappings. If negotiation state is lost, use `RestartGroupCallConnection` rather than guessing the next sequence. Restart replaces the device's connection generation without making it leave and rejoin.

In a [direct call](/schema/direct-calls), the original caller offers, the callee answers, and each endpoint trickles typed ICE candidates and end-of-candidates markers to the other. In a group call, DTLS-SRTP terminates at the SFU: media is **not end-to-end encrypted (E2EE)** between participants. Keep SDP, credentials, and track mappings out of shared state and logs.

## Moderation and presentation

Use the roster to distinguish a participant's effective role, restrictions, raised hand, and reported media state. A listener can raise a hand without publishing. Promoting a listener or lifting a mute restriction can permit publishing; it cannot remotely activate the microphone. The same boundary applies to the camera and screen capture.

Voice calls allow microphone audio only. In a video-enabled group call that permits screen sharing, the presenting device obtains local capture permission and submits a fresh presentation ID to `StartCallPresentation`. Negotiate presentation tracks under the admitted ID. Stop forwarding those tracks when the presentation ends; do not wait for another offer. A subscription never instructs the presenter to start capture.

## History and private feedback

`ListCallHistory` reads the authenticated account's terminal direct and group calls. A group entry is for someone who actually participated, not everyone in the room. Rejoins belong to the same entry. `DeleteCallHistory` removes only that account's row across its devices; it does not delete the conversation summary or anyone else's history.

`SetCallRating` sets the participant's private rating. `SubmitCallDiagnostics` requires separate, informed consent for each submission and accepts only the defined aggregate measurements, never raw logs or SDP. Neither rating nor diagnostics appear in shared updates or conversation messages.

This contract does not include recording or shareable call links. See the [generated reference](/schema/reference) for method and field definitions; the schema does not establish runtime availability.
